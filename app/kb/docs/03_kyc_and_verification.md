# Account Verification (KYC)

## Verification tiers and what each unlocks
Tier 1 (identity verified) allows deposits up to 10,000 USD per month and withdrawals up to 2,000 USD per day. Tier 2 (identity and proof of address verified) allows deposits up to 100,000 USD per month and withdrawals up to 10,000 USD per day. Tier 3 (enhanced due diligence with source of funds) removes the monthly deposit cap and allows withdrawals up to 50,000 USD per day.

## Documents we accept
Proof of identity: a valid passport, national ID card or driving licence, plus a short selfie liveness check. Proof of address: a utility bill or bank statement dated within the last 3 months showing your full name and residential address. Documents must be in colour, uncropped, with all four corners visible. Phone screenshots and edited files are rejected.

## How long verification takes
Most documents are reviewed within 1 to 2 business days. Enhanced due diligence for Tier 3 can take up to 5 business days because the compliance team reviews source-of-funds evidence manually.

## Common reasons a document is rejected
The most common reasons are a blurry or cropped image, an expired document, a name that does not match the account, a proof of address older than 3 months, and a document type we do not accept. You can re-upload at any time from the Verification page.

## Source of funds
For Tier 3, provide evidence of where your trading funds come from, such as recent payslips, bank statements, a sale agreement, or tax returns. Accepted documents depend on the source of the funds.
