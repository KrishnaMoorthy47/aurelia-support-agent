# Margin and Leverage

## Maximum leverage for retail clients
Retail clients are offered the following maximum leverage: major forex pairs 1:30, minor and exotic forex pairs 1:20, gold and major indices 1:20, other commodities 1:10, and cryptocurrency CFDs 1:2. Professional clients may apply for higher leverage, up to 1:200, if they meet the professional-client criteria and complete an additional assessment. Higher leverage increases both potential gains and potential losses.

## How required margin is calculated
Required margin equals contract size multiplied by lots multiplied by price, divided by leverage, expressed in the account currency. For example, 1 lot of EUR/USD (contract size 100,000) at a price of 1.1000 with 1:30 leverage requires (100,000 x 1.1000) / 30 = 3,666.67 USD of margin.

## Margin level, margin call and stop-out
Margin level is equity divided by used margin, shown as a percentage. When the margin level falls to 100% you receive a margin call warning. When it falls to 50% the platform automatically closes positions, starting with the largest losing position, until the margin level is back above 50%. This is called stop-out.

## Negative balance protection
Retail clients cannot lose more than the funds in their account. If market gaps cause the balance to go negative, Aurelia restores it to zero. This protection does not apply to professional clients.
