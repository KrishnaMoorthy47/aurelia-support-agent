# Deposits and Withdrawals

## Deposit methods and timing
Card deposits are credited instantly. E-wallet deposits are credited within 1 hour. Bank wire deposits take 1 to 3 business days. The minimum deposit is 100 USD for Standard accounts and 500 USD for Raw accounts.

## Withdrawal processing
Withdrawal requests approved before the 14:00 server time cut-off on a business day are processed the same day; later requests are processed the next business day. Bank wires then take 1 to 3 business days to reach your bank. The minimum withdrawal is 20 USD, or 100 USD for bank wire.

## Return-to-source rule
Withdrawals are paid back to the method used to deposit, up to the amount deposited with that method. Profits above that amount can be withdrawn to a bank account in your own name. We do not pay out to third-party accounts.

## Withdrawal limits and exception requests
Daily withdrawal limits depend on your verification tier. A customer who needs a temporary higher daily limit can request a limit exception. Exceptions are reviewed by the compliance team, need a written reason, and are decided within 2 business days. An exception can never exceed the limit of the next verification tier; completing the next tier of verification is usually faster. Exception requests are only approved after a compliance reviewer signs them off.

## Cancelling a withdrawal
A withdrawal can be cancelled from the platform while its status is Pending. Once it is Processing it cannot be cancelled.

## Account closure
You can ask to close your account at any time. Open positions must be closed first and any balance withdrawn. Account closure requests are reviewed by the compliance team before the account is closed.
