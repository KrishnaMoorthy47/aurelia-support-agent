# UAE Account Opening and Verification

## Documents for UAE residents
UAE residents verify their identity with a valid Emirates ID (front and back) and a valid passport. UAE Pass can be used instead of uploading the Emirates ID images, but a passport is still required. Proof of address can be a utility bill from DEWA, SEWA, ADDC or another UAE utility, a telecom bill from e& (Etisalat) or du, or a bank statement, dated within the last 3 months. A tenancy contract registered with Ejari is also accepted if it is valid on the date you upload it.

## Clients living outside the UAE
Clients who do not live in the UAE verify with a valid passport and a proof of address from their country of residence dated within the last 3 months. Aurelia does not accept clients who live in countries on its restricted list, which is shown during sign-up.

## Names that are spelled differently
Arabic names are often transliterated differently on an Emirates ID, a passport and a bank statement (for example Mohammed, Muhammad or Mohamed). Aurelia accepts the documents when they clearly belong to the same person, with the same date of birth and ID numbers. If the difference is larger, the compliance team may ask for an extra document before approving the account.

## Why we ask about your source of funds
UAE anti-money laundering law, now Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025, which replaced Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018, requires brokers to know their clients, understand where their money comes from and keep monitoring accounts. Aurelia may therefore ask where your deposits come from, for example employment income, savings, the sale of a property or a business, at any time and not only at sign-up. If the information is not provided, Aurelia may pause deposits or withdrawals until the review is finished.

## Questions support cannot answer about reviews
If an account is under a compliance review, support can tell you that a review is in progress and which documents are still needed. Support cannot share the reasons for a compliance review or whether a report has been made to the authorities, because the law does not allow it.

## Appropriateness assessment before trading CFDs
Before a retail client can trade CFDs, they answer a short questionnaire about their trading knowledge and experience. If the result shows that CFDs may not be appropriate, Aurelia explains the risks and recommends the demo account; the client can retake the questionnaire after 30 days.

## How long UAE verification takes
Most UAE applications with an Emirates ID or UAE Pass are reviewed within 1 business day. Applications that need a source-of-funds review can take up to 5 business days.
