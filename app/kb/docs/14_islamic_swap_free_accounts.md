# Islamic (Swap-Free) Accounts

## What a swap-free account is
An Islamic account is a swap-free trading account: Aurelia does not charge or pay overnight swap (financing) on positions held past the 22:00 server time rollover. It is available as a version of both the Standard and the Raw account, with the same spreads, commissions and leverage as the normal version.

## Administration fee on longer positions
Instead of swap, a flat administration fee of 4 USD per lot per night applies to forex positions held for more than 3 nights. Positions closed within 3 nights pay no administration fee. The fee is shown in the platform as a separate line, not as swap, so you can see exactly what was charged.

## Who can open an Islamic account
Any verified client can ask for an Islamic account. The request is made from the client area after verification is complete, and it applies to new trading accounts; an existing account can be switched only when it has no open positions. Aurelia does not ask clients to prove their religion.

## How long a request takes and what can change it
Islamic account requests are usually approved within 1 business day. Aurelia can switch an account back to a standard swap account, after giving notice, if the account is used mainly to earn from interest-rate differences between currencies rather than for trading.

## Swap-free accounts and Arabic support
UAE clients can make the request and get help with it in Arabic or English, in chat or by email. Support can explain how the account and the administration fee work, but cannot give a religious opinion on whether a product is compliant with Sharia.
