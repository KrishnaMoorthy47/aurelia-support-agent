# Risk Disclosure

## Risks of trading CFDs
Contracts for difference (CFDs) are complex instruments and carry a high risk of losing money rapidly because of leverage. A majority of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money.

## No investment advice
Aurelia support does not provide investment advice, personal recommendations, trading signals or price predictions. Nothing a support agent says should be treated as a reason to open or close a position. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

## Using a demo account
Aurelia offers free demo accounts with virtual funds so you can learn how the platform works without risking real money. Results on a demo account do not reflect real market conditions such as slippage and variable spreads.
