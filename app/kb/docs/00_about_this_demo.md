# About Aurelia Markets (demo)

## What this is
Aurelia Markets is a fictional online CFD broker invented for a software demonstration. Every fee, limit, policy, timeline and contact detail in these documents is made up. Nothing here describes a real company, and nothing here is financial advice.

## What support can and cannot do
Aurelia support can explain the broker's fees, margin rules, verification steps, deposit and withdrawal rules, and complaints process, and can look up the status of the signed-in customer's own account. Support cannot give investment advice, recommend trades, predict prices, or promise returns.
