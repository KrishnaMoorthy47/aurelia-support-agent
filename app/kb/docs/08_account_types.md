# Account Types

## Standard, Raw and Islamic accounts
The Standard account has no commission and a minimum deposit of 100 USD. The Raw account has tighter spreads plus commission and a minimum deposit of 500 USD. The Islamic (swap-free) account is available on both and charges no swap; instead a flat administration fee of 4 USD per lot per night applies to forex positions held for more than 3 nights. Islamic accounts must be requested after verification is complete.

## Base currencies and number of accounts
Account base currencies are USD, EUR, GBP and AED, and the base currency cannot be changed after the account is opened. Each client can hold up to 5 trading accounts.

## Demo accounts
Demo accounts start with 100,000 USD of virtual funds, never expire, and can be reset at any time from the platform.
