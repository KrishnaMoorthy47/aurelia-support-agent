# Platform, Trading Hours and Support

## Platforms
Aurelia offers the Aurelia Web Terminal and the Aurelia Mobile app for iOS and Android. Both use the same login and show the same account.

## Trading hours
Forex and gold trade from Sunday 22:00 to Friday 22:00 server time (UTC). Index CFD hours follow the underlying exchange and are listed in each instrument's specification. Cryptocurrency CFDs trade 24 hours a day, with a weekly maintenance window on Saturday between 02:00 and 03:00 server time.

## Support hours and channels
Live chat is available 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday. Email support is monitored every day and replies within 1 business day. Support can explain policies and look up your own account, but cannot change trades or give investment advice.

## Platform outages
If the trading platform is unavailable for more than 30 minutes, you can raise a ticket and the case is reviewed. Compensation is not automatic and depends on the review.
