# Fees and Charges

## Trading costs by account type
Standard accounts have no commission; the cost is built into the spread. Typical Standard spreads start from 1.0 pip on EUR/USD and from 0.30 USD on gold (XAU/USD). Raw accounts have tighter spreads, starting from 0.1 pip on EUR/USD, plus a commission of 3.50 USD per lot per side (7.00 USD per round-turn lot). Spreads are variable and widen around news releases and market open or close.

## Swap (overnight financing)
Positions held past 22:00 server time are charged or credited a swap rate, which depends on the instrument and the direction of the position. Swap on Wednesday is charged three times to cover the weekend. Current swap rates are shown in the platform's instrument specification.

## Deposit fees
Aurelia charges no fee for deposits by bank wire, card or e-wallet. Your bank or payment provider may charge its own fees, which Aurelia does not control.

## Withdrawal fees
The first withdrawal in each calendar month is free. Each additional withdrawal in the same month costs 10 USD for bank wire. Card withdrawals and e-wallet withdrawals have no Aurelia fee. For cryptocurrency withdrawals the network fee is passed through at cost.

## Currency conversion
If you deposit in a currency different from your account's base currency, a conversion fee of 0.5% applies to the converted amount.

## Inactivity fee
If an account has no trading activity for 12 consecutive months and still holds a balance, a fee of 10 USD per month is charged until the account becomes active again or the balance reaches zero. The fee never takes the balance below zero.
