# UAE Regulation and Client Money

## Who regulates Aurelia in the UAE
In this demo, Aurelia Markets serves UAE clients through an onshore UAE entity supervised by the UAE Capital Market Authority (CMA). The CMA was previously called the Securities and Commodities Authority (SCA); it was renamed on 1 January 2026 under Federal Decree-Law No. 32 of 2025, and licences issued under the SCA remain valid. Aurelia is fictional, so the licence number shown on the website (DEMO-0000) is a placeholder and does not appear on any real register.

## Financial free zones: DIFC and ADGM
The UAE has two financial free zones with their own regulators. Firms in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) are regulated by the Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA), and firms in Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) are regulated by the Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA). Aurelia's UAE entity is onshore, so the CMA, not the DFSA or FSRA, supervises it.

## How to check that a broker is licensed
Before you deposit with any broker, check the regulator's public register yourself: the CMA register for onshore firms, the DFSA register for DIFC firms and the FSRA register for ADGM firms. Never rely only on a licence number shown on a website, in an advert or in a social media message. Aurelia staff will never ask you to send money to a personal bank account or a crypto wallet.

## How your money is protected
Client money is held in segregated client bank accounts with banks licensed in the UAE, separately from Aurelia's own money. Aurelia may not use client money to run its business or to pay its creditors. Client money balances are reconciled every business day, and an independent auditor reviews the client money arrangements once a year.

## What segregation does and does not protect
Segregation protects your money if Aurelia itself becomes insolvent, because client money is kept apart from the firm's own assets. It does not protect you against trading losses, and it is not a guarantee of any return. Retail clients also have negative balance protection, so they cannot lose more than the funds in their account.

## Retail and professional clients in the UAE
Every new client is classified as a retail client by default. A client may ask to be treated as a professional client if they pass Aurelia's professional-client assessment of trading experience, financial knowledge and net assets. Professional clients can access leverage up to 1:200 but lose retail protections, including the retail leverage limits and negative balance protection.

## Deposits and withdrawals in AED
UAE clients can open accounts with AED as the base currency and can deposit by local UAE bank transfer in AED. Local AED transfers received before 13:00 Gulf Standard Time on a UAE business day are credited the same day; later transfers are credited the next business day. UAE business days are Monday to Friday. Aurelia charges no fee for AED bank deposits.
