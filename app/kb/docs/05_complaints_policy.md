# Complaints Policy

## How to make a complaint
You can complain through the support chat, or by email to complaints@aurelia-markets.example. Please include your account number, what went wrong, and what outcome you would like. Complaints are logged as a ticket the moment they are received, whatever the topic.

## What happens next
Aurelia sends an acknowledgement within 2 business days. A final written response is sent within 15 business days. If more time is needed we write to you before the deadline to explain why, and the response is never later than 35 business days.

## Escalation
If you are not satisfied with the response, you can ask for your complaint to be escalated to the Head of Compliance, who reviews it independently of the team that handled it first.

## External review
If you are still not satisfied after the final response, you can refer your complaint to the independent financial ombudsman service named in the final response letter. Referring a complaint there is free for customers.
