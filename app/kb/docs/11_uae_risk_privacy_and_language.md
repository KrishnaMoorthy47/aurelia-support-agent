# UAE Risk Warning, Data Privacy and Language

## Risk warning for UAE retail clients
CFDs are complex instruments and carry a high risk of losing money rapidly because of leverage. 71% of Aurelia's retail client accounts lost money when trading CFDs over the last 12 months. This figure is a demo value; it is recalculated every 3 months and shown on the website and in the platform.

## How Aurelia uses your personal data
For clients onshore in the UAE, Aurelia processes personal data under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021. Aurelia uses your data to open and run your account, to meet anti-money laundering and regulatory duties, and, only if you agree, to send you marketing. Aurelia does not sell personal data.

## Your data rights
You can ask for a copy of your personal data, ask for wrong data to be corrected, object to marketing at any time, and ask for data to be deleted where the law allows it. Send requests to privacy@aurelia-markets.example; Aurelia replies within 30 days.

## How long records are kept
Aurelia must keep identity documents and transaction records for at least 5 years after the account is closed, because anti-money laundering law requires it. These records cannot be deleted before that period ends, even if you ask.

## Support in Arabic and English
Aurelia support answers in Arabic and in English, in chat and by email. You can upload documents in Arabic or English. The client agreement is available in both languages; if the two versions differ, the English version applies.

## Complaints from UAE clients
UAE clients use the same complaints process as all clients: a ticket is logged at once, acknowledged within 2 business days and answered within 15 business days. If you are not satisfied with Aurelia's final response, you can raise the complaint with the UAE Capital Market Authority (CMA), the regulator that supervises Aurelia's UAE entity.
